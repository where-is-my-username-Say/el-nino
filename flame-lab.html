    Limitations and honesty notes

    • 2-D planar geometry. A planar slot flame has no steady microgravity state in two dimensions, so a microgravity flame keeps growing while it runs. Real droplet and jet flames are three-dimensional.
    • Not validated. Solver constants (fuel properties, heat-loss coefficient, vorticity confinement) are approximate and uncited. Hydrogen and low-oxygen results are unverified.
    • Visual detail is not physics. Fine texture is advected noise added for clarity. Colours are an illustrative temperature ramp.
    • UI heating and motion are a lumped model. Panel temperatures and motion follow the nearby gas, but they are an interface effect, not a heat-transfer calculation.
